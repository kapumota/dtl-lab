### Universal declarations

#### Data availability

Estado:

`DRAFT`

La versión final debe identificar, una vez publicados:

- source code;
- TLA+ models;
- Alloy models;
- frozen experiment specification;
- preserved raw observations;
- derived analysis artifacts;
- analysis scripts;
- reproduction scripts;
- integrity manifests;
- release/tag;
- DOI o URL persistente, si existe.

No inventar DOI, URL o release.

#### Generative AI and AI-assisted technologies

Estado:

`REQUIRED_FINAL_REVIEW`

Borrador de trabajo:

During the preparation of this work, the author(s) used ChatGPT (OpenAI) to assist with manuscript organization, drafting support, language refinement, and consistency and audit checks. The author(s) independently reviewed and edited the resulting content, verified the references, methods, experimental results, and scientific claims, and take full responsibility for the content of the article.

La redacción final debe ajustarse a la política vigente de la revista/publisher objetivo antes del envío.

#### Human participants / animals / personal data

Estado:

`CONFIRM_NOT_APPLICABLE`

Confirmar explícitamente antes del submission.

#### Originality and concurrent submission

Estado:

`REQUIRED_CONFIRMATION`

Confirmar antes de cada envío que:

- el manuscrito no está bajo revisión simultánea en otra revista;
- no ha sido publicado previamente como artículo sustancialmente equivalente;
- cualquier preprint, software release o artifact público se declara conforme a la política del target.
